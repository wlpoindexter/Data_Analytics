Northwind Review Notes

Categories
- Primary Key: CategoryID
- A record represents one product category.
- Useful columns: CategoryID, CategoryName.
- Not useful columns: Description and Picture because they are long text/binary fields.

Customers
- Primary Key: CustomerID
- A record represents one customer/company.
- Useful columns: CustomerID, CompanyName, ContactName, ContactTitle, City, Region, PostalCode, Country.
- Address, Phone, and Fax are not needed for Power BI analysis.

Employees
- Primary Key: EmployeeID
- A record represents one employee.
- Useful columns: EmployeeID, LastName, FirstName, Title, TitleOfCourtesy, BirthDate, HireDate, City, Region, PostalCode, Country, ReportsTo.
- BirthDate can be used to calculate employee age. HireDate can be used to calculate years employed.
- Photo, Notes, HomePhone, Extension, and PhotoPath are not useful for this model.

EmployeeTerritories
- Primary Key: EmployeeID and TerritoryID
- Foreign Keys: EmployeeID points to Employees; TerritoryID points to Territories.
- A record represents an employee assigned to a sales territory.
- Useful for connecting employees to territories.

Order Details
- Primary Key: OrderID and ProductID
- Foreign Keys: OrderID points to Orders; ProductID points to Products.
- A record represents one product line item in an order.
- Useful columns: OrderID, ProductID, UnitPrice, Quantity, Discount.
- UnitPrice, Quantity, and Discount can be used to calculate Sales Amount and Discount Amount.

Orders
- Primary Key: OrderID
- Foreign Keys: CustomerID points to Customers; EmployeeID points to Employees; ShipVia points to Shippers.
- A record represents one customer order.
- Useful columns: OrderID, CustomerID, EmployeeID, OrderDate, RequiredDate, ShippedDate, ShipVia, Freight, ShipName, ShipCity, ShipRegion, ShipPostalCode, ShipCountry.
- ShipAddress is not needed because street-level address is too detailed for this report.

Products
- Primary Key: ProductID
- Foreign Keys: SupplierID points to Suppliers; CategoryID points to Categories.
- A record represents one product sold by Northwind.
- Useful columns: ProductID, ProductName, SupplierID, CategoryID, QuantityPerUnit, UnitPrice, UnitsInStock, UnitsOnOrder, ReorderLevel, Discontinued.
- UnitPrice and inventory columns can be used for product and inventory analysis.

Region
- Primary Key: RegionID
- A record represents a sales region.
- Useful columns: RegionID, RegionDescription.

Shippers
- Primary Key: ShipperID
- A record represents one shipping company.
- Useful columns: ShipperID and CompanyName.
- Phone is not needed for analysis.

Suppliers
- Primary Key: SupplierID
- A record represents one product supplier.
- Useful columns: SupplierID, CompanyName, ContactName, ContactTitle, City, Region, PostalCode, Country.
- Address, Phone, Fax, and HomePage are not needed for analysis.

Territories
- Primary Key: TerritoryID
- Foreign Key: RegionID points to Region.
- A record represents one sales territory.
- Useful columns: TerritoryID, TerritoryDescription, RegionID.
